# ROS-Rabi-UPE Model v1.1 update

This update adds the completed spectral and temporal analyses developed for the revised manuscript.

## Added analyses
- Experimental-basis spectral analysis using digitized Tsuchida & Kobayashi spectra.
- Effective Franck-Condon vibronic treatment of the carbonyl emission channel.
- Temporal onset, peak-time, delay, and optical-compression analysis.
- Detector-forward spectral analysis separating intrinsic and instrument-weighted spectra.
- Literature-integrated Discussion.
- Manuscript-wide terminology revision distinguishing ensemble spectral redistribution from intrinsic molecular wavelength shifts.

## Key interpretation boundary
Disease-specific UPE magnitudes and spectra are structurally constrained computational predictions. The model does not claim that infection shifts the intrinsic transition wavelength of an individual emitter. Disease-associated spectral changes arise from altered relative contributions of fixed emission channels.

## Experimental basis data
Digitized spectra reconstructed from published normalized curves should be treated as approximate empirical basis functions until original numerical data are obtained from the corresponding authors.
